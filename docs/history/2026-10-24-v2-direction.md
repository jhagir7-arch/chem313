# Historical v2 direction (2026-10-24)

This public summary preserves the product direction, not a current feature or release claim.
The proposed migration was from the older Swift/macOS and browser-based portable shells to
Tauri, React and a local Python engine. Its intended workflow was structure, geometry,
setup, results and saved records, with a scientific instrument-style interface.

Long-term proposals included educational guidance, a persistent job queue, accessibility,
optional engine packs, signing and cross-platform native verification. They were goals,
not implemented commitments. See the community handoff for current scope and gaps.
