# Chem313 community guidance

Chem313 welcomes public, reproducible feedback through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jhagir7-arch/openchem/issues). There is no email support channel in this preview.

## Helpful reports

- Describe the beginner task you tried, what you expected, and what happened.
- Include the preview version, operating system, architecture, and steps that reproduce the problem.
- Flag keyboard focus, tab order, visible focus, shortcuts, labels, contrast, reading order, and screen-reader problems.
- Use synthetic or rights-cleared molecules and data. Remove private paths, credentials, student records, research data, and licensed-engine files.

## Review boundaries

Chemistry reviewers can check whether interface wording accurately distinguishes force-field results, prepared inputs, recorded outputs, and real external-engine runs. Include the relevant inputs, units, method label, assumptions, and source. Keep uncertainty visible: a displayed value, normal exit, or test fixture is not proof of convergence, experimental agreement, or a quantum calculation.

## Release boundaries

Platform and release reports should state the exact OS, architecture, package, and validation evidence. New runtimes, dependencies, binaries, models, fixtures, and data require a redistribution and notice review before inclusion. The current Mac preview is ad-hoc signed and unnotarized. Windows and Linux artifacts are not offered until their native smoke, licensing, and notice gates are recorded.

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Report security-sensitive material through the route in [SECURITY.md](../release/public-source/SECURITY.md), without publishing secrets or exploit details.
